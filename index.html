CONCEPT PREVIEW · COMMUNITY FEEDBACK

Meet Nimva

A scheduling app that manages your energy across the week — not just your time. Built for students who do a lot and want to do it without burning out.

📋 Your feedback directly shapes what gets built next. Nothing here is final.
The idea
Scheduling apps manage time. Nimva manages energy.
Most apps tell you what to do and when. Nimva asks how draining each thing is, then builds your week so the heavy stuff is spread out — protecting you from hitting a wall mid-week.
